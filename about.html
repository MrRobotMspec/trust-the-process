About Us

Mind, body, breath, movement — and spirit.

We believe wellness is not only about physical fitness. It is about awareness, balance, resilience and inner wellbeing — cultivated carefully, over time.

Our Story

Built by practitioners, for practitioners.

Rooted Movement began as a small studio with one belief: that movement, taught carefully, can change how a person feels in their body and in their life. Not through intensity for its own sake — but through precision, patience and presence.

What started as a handful of yoga and Pilates classes has grown into a complete practice — integrating mobility, strength, flexibility, breath and meditation under one roof. The studio is deliberately small. We want to know your name, your injuries, your goals and your quiet victories.

Beginnings

A single room, a single mat

The first classes were taught to six students in a sunlit room with borrowed props.

Growth

Pilates and mobility added

As students asked for more, we expanded — bringing in specialist teachers in core strength and joint mobility.

Today

A holistic studio

Yoga, Pilates, mobility, strength, flexibility and spiritual wellness — taught as one integrated practice.

Our Philosophy

Five threads, one practice.

We approach every class through the same lens — that mind, body, breath, movement and spirit are not separate. They inform each other constantly.

◐

Mind

Attention, curiosity and non-judgement. We teach you to notice sensation before chasing a shape.

◍

Body

Strength, mobility and alignment. The physical practice is honest, progressive and built to last.

◌

Breath

Breath is the bridge. It steadies the nervous system and deepens every posture and movement.

✦

Movement

Quality over quantity. Functional, controlled movement that carries into everyday life.

❋

Spirit

Stillness, reflection and space. Not belief — practice. A quiet centre you return to.

◈

Community

A small studio where people know each other, encourage each other and grow together.

Our Approach

Careful teaching. No shortcuts.

Every class is designed with intention. We do not chase trends, and we do not substitute intensity for skill. Instead, we build practice gradually — beginning with breath, layering in mobility, and only then loading strength.

Teachers at Rooted Movement train continuously in anatomy, cueing, and modification. They adjust. They observe. They meet you where you are — whether that is your first class or your thousandth.

Wellness is not a state you achieve. It is a relationship you build — with your body, your breath, and your attention.

Our Community

A studio that knows your name.

Rooted Movement is intentionally small. We cap class sizes so teachers can actually teach, and students can actually be seen. Over time, a community forms — one that shows up for each other, on good days and difficult ones.

Whether you come for the movement, the stillness, or simply the hour of quiet in your week — you will find a place here.
